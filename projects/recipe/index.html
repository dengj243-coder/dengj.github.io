THIS IS A VERY SERIOUS FLIGHT EXPERIMENT

HOW TO MAKE A
PAPER AIRPLANE ✈

One piece of paper.
Six simple steps.
Let's see how far it can fly.

WHAT YOU NEED

01 PIECE OF PAPER

HOW TO MAKE IT

01

Fold the paper in half.

02

Open the paper and fold the top two corners to the center line.

03

Fold the top edges to the center again.

04

Fold the whole paper in half.

05

Fold both sides down to make the wings.

06

Hold the airplane in the middle and throw it!
